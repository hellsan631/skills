# Owner's quality bar

- This skill is specifically for design work. Center its triggers, instructions, and deliverables on designing game screens; implementation and QA are not its purpose.
- The owner expects this skill to teach what makes a strong game screen: gameplay priorities, player fantasy, composition, visual hierarchy, and feedback. Workflow and usability checks alone do not meet that goal.
- Judge design guidance against rendered screens. Prose lint and written scenario answers establish neither visual quality nor a game-worthy result; report those limits when evaluating the skill.
